Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start my US/Schengen visa application?

We recommend starting the process at least 3 to 6 months prior to your intended travel date. This accommodates waitlists for appointment dates, documentation collection, and processing delays.

What happens if my visa has been refused in the past?

A refusal does not permanently block travels. We conduct a complete audit of your refusal letters and DS-160 forms, redesign your travel strategy, and address the specific consulate objections in your new cover letters.

Do you guarantee visa approvals?

Legally, only consular officers have the right to grant visas. While no agency can guarantee approvals, our auditing framework and structured interview prep result in a consistent 99% approval rating.
