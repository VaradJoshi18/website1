Our Story

Crafting pathways to the extraordinary since 2010

Pleasure Global Travel & Visa LLP was founded with a singular, clear vision: to redefine luxury travel planning. What began as a boutique advisory service in Florida has flourished into a globally respected concierge enterprise, offering customized routing, logistics, and visas for discerning corporate leaders and leisure explorers.

We operate under the philosophy that time is the ultimate luxury. Our clients trust us to manage every operational obstacle—whether securing complex multi-destination visas, coordinating chauffeured transfers across remote districts, or arranging private cabin bookings.
